Kyodo Privacy Policy

Kyodo is built by medical doctors and researchers for unpublished scientific manuscripts, clinical trials, and grant proposals. Your data sovereignty is foundational to our architecture.

1
100% Local Manuscript & Vault Processing

Your unpublished manuscripts, PDFs, and local citation graphs stay on your Mac. Kyodo never sells, rents, or trains generative models on your research data.

2
Zero Screen Recording & Zero Keylogging

Kyodo never captures desktop pixels or records your screen (0 pixels captured). It never requests macOS Screen Recording permissions.

3
Browser Companion Extension (nativeMessaging, activeTab, storage)

The Chrome/Brave/Edge extension communicates strictly with your local macOS Kyodo app over standard stdio framing to resolve academic DOIs, clip open-access papers into your local Vault, and ground scientific claims in Google Docs.

4
Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) & Managed Cloud Calls

When you link your own Google Gemini key or use Managed LiveCite/LiveChat synthesis, only the specific claim snippet or selected paper passage needed for verification is transmitted over TLS.
